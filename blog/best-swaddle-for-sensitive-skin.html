What "Hypoallergenic" Actually Means

"Hypoallergenic" doesn't mean the fabric is magic — it means it's designed to be gentler and less likely to trigger a reaction. For a swaddle, that comes down to a few things: natural fibers, fewer harsh chemicals, and a weave that lets skin breathe.

Newborn skin is thinner and more reactive than adult skin. It loses moisture faster, and it soaks up whatever it touches more easily. So a fabric that's soft and breathable isn't a nice-to-have. It's the difference between calm skin and a fussy, uncomfortable baby.

Why Bamboo Is Gentler on Sensitive Skin

We chose 95% bamboo rayon for every Sincerely Cozy swaddle for exactly this reason. Bamboo has a few qualities that make it especially kind to delicate skin.

It's naturally smooth. Bamboo fibers are long and round, with no rough edges to rub against the skin. When your baby spends hours wrapped up, that smoothness is what keeps their skin calm instead of chafed.

It's breathable. Bamboo lets air circulate and pulls moisture away from the skin. That helps prevent the heat-and-sweat combination that so often leads to irritation and rashes.

It's hypoallergenic. Bamboo is naturally resistant to dust mites and mold, and it's gentle enough for the most sensitive skin — including babies prone to eczema. Less irritation means fewer red patches, and fewer red patches means more sleep for everyone.

What to Look For in a Swaddle for Sensitive Skin

If you're shopping for a baby with sensitive skin, here's what actually matters.

Check the fiber content. Look for a high percentage of natural bamboo or cotton — not synthetic blends that trap heat and can irritate.

Skip the harsh finishes. Opt for fabrics without added dyes or heavy chemical treatments. Fewer additives, fewer chances for a reaction.

Prioritize breathability. A swaddle that traps heat and moisture is a recipe for red, angry skin. Breathable fabric is the single simplest thing you can choose.

Mind the size. A generous square — like our 47" × 47" — wraps comfortably without having to be pulled tight, so there's no added pressure on delicate skin.
