Frequently Asked Questions

What are your swaddles made of?

95% rayon from bamboo and 5% spandex for softness plus stretch. Every Sincerely Cozy swaddle uses this same blend. To see how bamboo compares with other fabrics, read Why Bamboo for Baby Swaddles and Bamboo vs. Muslin Swaddle.

What size is a Sincerely Cozy swaddle?

Each swaddle measures 34" × 40". It is one size across all six prints. For more on size versus fit, see What Size Swaddle Do You Need?.

What prints are available?

There are six prints: Baby Blue, Cozy Teddy Bear, Dreamy Clouds, Little Butterfly, Pickaboo Bear and Rose Petal. You can see them all on the swaddle collection page.

What comes in the box with my swaddle?

It depends on the print. Little Butterfly, Rose Petal and Pickaboo Bear ship with a bow headband. Baby Blue, Cozy Teddy Bear and Dreamy Clouds ship with a knotted hat. Each product page states which accessory comes with that print.

How do I wash a Sincerely Cozy swaddle?

Machine wash cold with like colors on a gentle cycle, using a mild detergent. Tumble dry low or lay flat to dry, and avoid bleach and high heat. The full routine, with more detail, is on our care instructions page and in the Bamboo Swaddle Care Guide.

Does the swaddle stay soft after washing?

Sincerely Cozy designs the swaddle to stay soft after repeated washes, and following the care steps above is how you protect that softness. Customers on Amazon also describe the blanket as very soft and stretchy.

When should I stop swaddling?

When your baby shows signs of rolling, transition to arms-out sleep for safety. Our guide How to Transition Out of Swaddling covers this milestone, and Baby Sleep Safety 101 has our sleep safety guidance.

What else can I use the swaddle for?

It is a multi-use blanket. Besides swaddling, it works as a nursing cover, stroller canopy, burp cloth and tummy time blanket. See One Blanket, Many Uses for ideas.

Is the swaddle good for sensitive skin?

Sincerely Cozy describes its bamboo-rayon swaddles as hypoallergenic, breathable and lightweight. Our post The Best Swaddle for Sensitive Skin explains why we chose bamboo. If you have concerns about your baby's skin, ask your pediatrician.

Is the swaddle stretchy?

Yes. The 5% spandex in the blend gives the fabric stretch, which makes it easier to wrap. Our how-to-swaddle guide walks through the technique step by step.

How many swaddles do I need?

Our post How Many Swaddles Do You Need? helps you decide how many to keep on hand.

Is the swaddle breathable and lightweight?

Yes. Sincerely Cozy describes its bamboo-rayon swaddles as breathable and lightweight, and customers on Amazon call the blanket "very light." For the reasons parents choose bamboo, see Why Bamboo for Baby Swaddles.

Do I need to wash the swaddle before the first use?

Our site does not give separate first-wash instructions. When you wash it, use the standard care steps: cold water, gentle cycle, mild detergent, and low heat or air drying. Details are on the care instructions page.

Can the headband be adjusted?

Customers who bought a print that ships with a headband report that it adjusts by tying. Only Little Butterfly, Rose Petal and Pickaboo Bear ship with a headband; Baby Blue, Cozy Teddy Bear and Dreamy Clouds ship with a knotted hat instead.

Where can I buy Sincerely Cozy swaddles?

Sincerely Cozy swaddles are sold on Amazon through our storefront. You can browse every print on the collection page and follow the links to Amazon from there.

Is shipping free?

Orders placed on Amazon ship free within the U.S.

Do you ship outside the U.S.?

At this time, Sincerely Cozy ships within the U.S. only.
