Bamboo Swaddle Blanket

Little Butterfly

Little Butterfly is the most colorful print in the collection. Watercolor-style butterflies in rose pink, soft coral and pale aqua drift across a creamy white background, with a few tiny flowers and petals scattered between them. The painted look keeps edges soft, so it feels delicate rather than loud. Some butterflies are large and detailed while others are tiny, which gives the pattern depth.

Underneath the print is the same fabric as the rest of the range: 95% bamboo rayon in a 47" × 47" square. It is lightweight and breathable, hypoallergenic, and stretchy, which helps with wrapping. It is also made to stay soft through repeated washing, so the soft feel that goes with the painted butterflies lasts.

Beyond swaddling, the square works as a nursing cover, stroller canopy, burp cloth or tummy time blanket. If you want the one with the most color in it, and still a pale, gentle backdrop, this is the print to look at first.
