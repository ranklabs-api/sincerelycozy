Bamboo Swaddle Blanket

Dreamy Clouds

Dreamy Clouds pairs white fabric with sketchy, hand-inked clouds in grey and navy outline, washes of aqua, and a sprinkling of small stars. The cool aqua-and-grey palette sits between the blue and pink prints, so it reads as calm without being tied to any one color scheme.

It is a 47" × 47" swaddle blanket made from 95% bamboo rayon. The fabric is breathable and lightweight, hypoallergenic, and has the stretch that makes wrapping easy. Because the fabric is lightweight, the large square never feels heavy, and the site notes that it stays soft after washes, so the soft aqua washes of the print are not the only thing that stays gentle.

A busy day usually asks a blanket to do several jobs. This one can serve as a swaddle, nursing cover, stroller canopy, burp cloth or tummy time blanket, and the cloud print is a good match for a sleepy-themed nursery.
